Site Asset Notes
Summary of Site Changes / Promo Overview:
SAC Bundle & Save — Buy 3+ Summer Items, Save an Extra 20% (October 20–October 26, 2026)

Campaign Synopsis:
Framed as an end-of-season summer clearout, this bundle promotion gives SAC customers an extra 20% off when they buy three or more eligible summer items. Eligible merch spans men's and women's summer apparel, hiking shoes, climbing gear, bike gear, sunglasses, camp and hike, fishing, and backpacks. The goal is to clear remaining summer inventory and drive multi-unit baskets as the site shifts to winter. The discount applies automatically in cart once 3+ eligible items are added — no promo code required. Messaging should lean on last-chance urgency: this is the final markdown push on summer gear.

Key Messages:
·      End-of-Season Summer Blowout
·      Buy 3+ summer items, save an extra 20%
·      Shop summer apparel, hiking shoes, climb, bike, sunglasses, camp & hike, fishing, and backpacks
·      Discount applies automatically in cart — no promo code needed
·      October 20–26 only — last chance on summer gear

Key Narrative:
