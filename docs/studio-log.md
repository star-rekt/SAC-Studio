# SAC Studio app — change log

The app (`/`, `src/studio/`): start or open a campaign, then Plan (each asset type, how many,
how many confirmed), Build (each asset customised and confirmed one by one), Export. It began
as a wireframe beside the old editor (`review.html`) and replaced it. Branch `email-sections` (the email sections have their own log,
`docs/email-sections-log.md`).

| Change | What | Files |
|---|---|---|
| Wireframe | Start page (new campaign from a brief, recent and all campaigns, import), the plan, the review window (copy with fit, the asset, its editing panel, confirm / confirm again), Image and Logo galleries in Local / Figma tabs, upload captions. | `review.html`, `src/review/*`, `src/edit/GalleryTabs.tsx`, `Picture.tsx`, `Media.tsx`, `styles.css` |
| Export | Every asset confirmed: an Export card on the plan -- **Export to Figma** (the plugin file, as the editor's) and **Download PNGs** (every asset rendered with its edits, one zip; new `GET /api/png/:session`). | `src/review/Review.tsx`, `src/session/store.ts` (`downloadPngs`), `server/export.ts` (`pngZip`), `server/index.ts`, `review.css` |
| Zoom | − / % / + / Fit all over the asset in the review window: steps of 25%, the % resets to the fit (an email by width), Fit all shows the whole asset. | `src/review/Review.tsx`, `review.css` |
| Review with AI | A floating button in the review window: Claude reviews the asset on screen (the studio's judge, `server/rules.md`, one call, ~20s) and shows the verdict and its issues, with layer ids read as names. No conversation; Look again asks again. | `src/review/AiReview.tsx`, `review.css` |
| The app | The wireframe becomes the app at `/` (`review.html` and `src/review/` → `src/studio/`; Review → Studio, its asset window → Build; classes `rv-` → `st-`), with Plan · Build · Export steps and the Figma-token prompt (asked once at start). The old editor is deleted (`src/App.tsx`), with the files only it used (`ai/Panel.tsx`, `ai/apply.ts`, `ai/reconcile.ts`, `edit/Marquee.tsx`), its exports (the Copy tab, the Open/New dialogs, `sheetHash`, `exportForFigma`) and 32 classes of its CSS. Its edit operations live on in `edit/useEditApi.ts`. Kept for the new editor's feature list (unused until then): `fontCheck` (missing fonts), `GroupRow` (alignment), `propose` (AI fixes). | `src/main.tsx`, `src/studio/*`, `src/edit/useEditApi.ts`, `src/edit/fieldFit.ts` (was `CopyFit.tsx`), `src/styles.css`, `scripts/check-three-boards.tsx` |
| Customise in Build | With nothing picked, Build's right panel is **Add**: + Text (a text layer, now drawn on any asset, not only custom boards), the graphics (click to add), and Upload a graphic (with tags; uploads show their tags as the caption, retag in place, × to remove). Uploads work everywhere in Build now (photos, logos, graphics): the app has the real library functions. Anything picked gets Reset, Remove (for what was added) and Done. | `src/studio/AddPanel.tsx` (new), `src/studio/Studio.tsx`, `src/render/CustomBoard.tsx` (`CustomTexts`), `src/render/One.tsx`, `src/edit/Media.tsx` (`UserTags` exported), `studio.css` |
| No design directions | Removed (your call): the A/B/C treatments per asset type. Everything drew direction A, which changed nothing, so nothing looks different (35 of 39 sampled assets pixel-identical; the other 4 never used directions and differ only by photos edited since). Gone: `templates/directions.ts`, the `direction` props (site formats, Bump socials, `Compose`, `AssetView`), the sheet's `direction` / `formatDirections`, the email header's alternates, the unused `Direction` panel and its CSS, and their checks. | `src/render/*`, `src/sheet/*`, `src/Shot.tsx`, `src/studio/Studio.tsx`, `scripts/check-three-boards.tsx` |
| Shared small-format designs | The small formats the templates draw differently can take another template's design, in the campaign's own colours and copy: category tiles (photo tile, or Bump's halftone), affiliate link (Flash's link row, or the banner bar), mobile hero (photo, or Bump's cream). Picked in Build's Design list; each template's own design is first and the default, so nothing changes until one is picked (39 sampled assets pixel-identical). The campaign banner, global banner, badges and text banners are one design in every template: nothing to borrow. | `templates/variants.ts` (`SHARED`, `siteDesign`), `render/One.tsx`, `templates/BoardExtras.tsx` (`siteAccent`, `design`), `bump/BumpTile.tsx` (`colour`) |
| Build's editing tools | Kept minimal: X/Y position for text and backgrounds; background shapes get their fill colour, size and position (they had been offered text colours); shift-click or **Select** (drag over the asset) picks several, then Left / Centre / Right aligns them; **Grid** over the asset with − / + for density; **Reset all** for the asset (asks twice). The stage now scales with CSS zoom (`.scaler`), the way Alt-drag and alignment measure it: under a transform they had moved things at the wrong rate. Box select (`edit/Marquee.tsx`) and the fix applier (`ai/apply.ts`) come back from git. | `studio/Studio.tsx`, `edit/Controls.tsx` (`GroupRow` made compact), `edit/Marquee.tsx`, `studio.css`, `styles.css` |
| Plan: the campaign | The name is edited in place; the template switches within its family (Bump ↔ Bump 2, Flash ↔ Named Sale), rebuilt from the brief with the edits kept; **+ Add an asset type** offers the template's types not in the campaign, or a custom one by name; − at one removes a type; a quiet note when brand font weights are missing. | `studio/Studio.tsx` (`replan`, `AddType`, `FAMILIES`), `studio.css` |
| Suggest fixes | In the Review with AI card, once there's a verdict with issues: **Suggest fixes** asks Claude what to move, resize, recolour or retype (`/ai/fix`), lists the changes in words and what it can't fix, and **Apply** makes them -- the same edits as by hand, so the asset needs confirming again. Nothing changes until Apply. Layer ids read as names (the asset's own id aside). | `studio/AiCheck.tsx`, `studio/Studio.tsx`, `ai/apply.ts`, `studio.css` |
| Build stays the quick pass | Build is copy, photos, logos, graphics (add, upload, tag, remove), text layers, grid, design and sections, confirm, Review with AI. The precision tools move to the expanded editor (to come): X/Y position, select several and align, backgrounds, Reset all are out of Build. `edit/Marquee.tsx` (box select) and `GroupRow` (align) wait for it, unused until then. The graphics scroll in their own box; the panel's end clears the AI button. | `studio/Studio.tsx`, `studio.css` |
| Fine-tune | The precise pass, opened from the overview (beside the steps), not from Build: every asset of the campaign by type in tabs (each with its confirmed count), one under another at the pane's width, with zoom. Click anything for colour, size and X/Y position (backgrounds included); shift-click or **Select** an area, then Left / Centre / Right; **Grid** with density; Reset an asset (asks twice). Each asset has Confirm, each tab Confirm all, so a campaign can go overview → Fine-tune → export without Build. The confirm rules and type names are shared (`studio/assets.ts`); an asset's signature now also covers graphics and text layers added to it (only where it has any, so earlier confirmations stand). | `studio/FineTune.tsx` (new), `studio/assets.ts` (new), `studio/Studio.tsx`, `studio.css` |
| Email and header are one | A send's email header is the top of its email. Named Sale's had been a design compiled on its own with its own ids, so an edit in one never reached the other (a photo changed in the email stayed the board's in the header). Now its header draws as its email's hero section under the email's ids -- one set of edits, as Bump's, Bump 2's and Flash's already were (all four checked: each header is its email's top, pixel for pixel). The 15 separately compiled header designs are gone from the template (lab: integrate no longer pairs them). On the plan, an email and its header change count together; confirming either covers the pair's edits, so an edit in either view asks for both again. | `named-sale/NamedSale.tsx` (`NamedSaleHero`), `named-sale/designs.ts` + `frames/` (15 header frames removed), `templates/variants.ts`, `studio/Studio.tsx`, `studio/assets.ts`; lab: `integrate.mjs` |
| Fine-tune is Build, precise | Fine-tune is now the same editor as Build in a precise mode, not a second editor: everything Build has (copy with fit, design, sections, Add, Review with AI with fixes, grid, confirm) plus the precise tools (X/Y position, backgrounds, Select and align, Reset an asset), every type in tabs, and a type's assets side by side (emails three across, the rest two; click into one to make it current). Opened from a button under the plan, in line with the Build buttons. `studio/FineTune.tsx` is gone. | `studio/Studio.tsx` (`Build` `fine`), `studio.css` |
| Suggestions only | Review with AI's suggested fixes are listed, never applied: Apply moved text badly on a Named Sale header (Wireframe test, E2). `ai/apply.ts` goes. Fine-tune's Reset on an email or its header resets the pair's edits (one set of edits). | `studio/AiCheck.tsx`, `studio/Studio.tsx`, `studio/assets.ts` |
