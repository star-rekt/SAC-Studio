# Named Sale — change log

A template for a sale named for its occasion, compiled whole from the SC26_0027 Memorial Day
board by the template lab (`template-lab/`, outside the repo). Branch `named-sale`, off
`template-lab`. The proxy trial was dropped (branch `figma-proxy` deleted; its last commit was
`9e12552`).

## What's in it
| Piece | File | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Frames | `src/render/templates/named-sale/frames/<frame>/` | One folder per board frame, as the lab generated it: the component (board drawn by `FigmaLayers`, editable parts as slots), `layout.json`, `nudges.ts`. 22 frames: 4 hero desktop, 3 socials, 15 email bodies. |
| Images | `public/figma-assets/named-sale/` | The frames' images and the lab's packaged SVGs (fixed art kept as vectors on export). 21 MB: integrate.mjs keeps each at most 1800px on its long side and re-encodes JPEGs at 72 (from the lab's originals), and leaves out pictures only removed layers drew (the "3 DAYS ONLY" sticker). |
| Lists | `named-sale/designs.ts` (data), `named-sale/components.ts` | Generated by `template-lab/integrate.mjs`. Every design's format, node, size and short name, and its component. |
| Router | `named-sale/NamedSale.tsx` | Draws an asset with its chosen design, else the format's first. |
| Spec | `src/templates/specs/named_sale.ts`, `src/templates/spec.ts` | "Named Sale" in the template picker, grounded in SC26_0027. Formats with no board frame use Bump 2's vocabulary. |
| Routing | `src/render/One.tsx` | Email bodies (by the send's design), hero desktop and socials (by the asset's design). |
| Designs | `src/templates/variants.ts` | The 15 email designs and 4 hero designs in the design pickers. |
| Send design | `src/sheet/build.ts` | A send whose angle names a design's urgency ("ends today", "final hours", "two days only") gets that design; the rest take the designs in turn. |
| Brief copy | (in the generated frames) | The sale's name lines ("MEMORIAL DAY SALE") take the brief's sale name; offer, its number ("60" beside "%"), lead ("UP TO"), urgency, code and CTAs take the brief's; brand logos the brief's brands; photos and product photos swap. |
| Brief type | `src/model/brief.ts` (`TYPE_WORDS`) | A sale named for its occasion (Memorial Day, Labor Day, Presidents' Day, Veterans Day, Independence Day, Black Friday, Cyber Monday, a holiday or anniversary sale) is read as a named sale before the bump and flash words, which a Memorial Day brief also has ('extra 20%', a code). "Labor Day Weekend Flash Sale" now reads as a named sale too. |
| Phases | `src/model/namedSale.ts`, `src/sheet/build.ts` | A named sale's phases each say their own offer, read off the brief's own sections. Main: up to N% off. Refresh: "More Brands, Deeper Discounts", NEW BRANDS ADDED. Bumps: extra N% off, each with its promo block's code, N DAYS ONLY / ENDS TODAY. Flash: up to N% off, N DAYS ONLY. An instance's phase comes from its words, not its place in the list. Each asset gets the board design drawn for its phase (designs.ts: `phase`, `ends`). A brief with a code whose words name no phase gets a design with a code line. Named-sale briefs only. |
| Email hero | `named-sale/NamedSale.tsx` (`NamedSaleHero`), `One.tsx` | A send's email hero is the top of its email design, cut where the hero ends (designs.ts `heroHeight`: its first section after the hero). Same component, same ids as the send's email body. |
| Export | `src/render/toLayers.ts` | Transparent type isn't exported. It's the copy that carries a grain over a headline, and it came in as a black duplicate. |
| Board check | `scripts/check-three-boards.tsx` | The smoke brief's offer is checked in the words as read (an offer set as "20" / "%" / "OFF" layers reads as one line). Named Sale's email hero is checked for the brief's offer number, not Claude's headline verbatim: its lockups have no headline line. |
| Email heroes | `template-lab/heroes.json`, `named-sale/frames/<hero>/` | Each email design's hero section, compiled as a design of its own and paired with its email by id (design 3's hero is design 3). Replaces the earlier crop of the email body. It has its own edits: a logo changed in the email doesn't change its hero. |
| Graphics | `FigmaLayers.tsx` (`FigmaMark`), `template-lab/codegen.mjs`, `named-sale/marks.json`, `src/data/mark-shapes.json`, `src/model/media.ts`, `public/graphics/named-sale-*.svg` | The board's graphics as library marks (recoloured, resized, swapped, exported as vector paths): the brush arrows as `bump2-arrow`; each pill (CTA, category button) as a mark of its own exact shape, its label and contents live over it; other one-colour art (offer brushes, bolts) as marks cut from the board's paths, its grain kept as an overlay. 50 marks, registered by integrate.mjs. |
| Renderer fixes | `FigmaLayers.tsx` | A boolean operation draws only its result (the bolts were gold: their operands drew over the white). A paragraph wraps at its board size (long copy was fitted as one line and shrank to 40%). A text's colour is its topmost fill ("Looking For More Deals?" was white on white). |
| Roles | `template-lab/codegen.mjs`, `roles.dictionary.json` | Only a generic CTA ("Shop Now", "Shop All") takes the brief's; "Shop Men's" keeps its words. Photo slots by size against the frame's width (category thumbnails, product cut-outs), named `…/photo`. The "3 DAYS ONLY" sticker removed by its picture, wherever it is. Brand names with a note ("Osprey *NEW") match the stocked brand. An unstocked brand gets a stocked placeholder logo. The sale's name is swapped inside its line ("MEMORIAL DAY FAVORITES" → "LABOR DAY FAVORITES"). |
| Compile | `scripts/figma-pull.ts`, `layout.ts` (`placeIn`) | A frame compiled on its own keeps its turn on the board. FULL: a mirrored layer (Figma reports a flip as a 180° turn) is recorded as `mirror`, apart from its turn, and placed and flipped as Figma draws it. Email design 13's hero is mirrored, with its type mirrored back: its photo showed the wrong part. Now 98% (email) and 98.4% (hero). |
| Copy movement | `FigmaLayers.tsx` (`copy`), `template-lab/codegen.mjs` (`COPY_MOVES`) | **Off for Named Sale** (review, 2026-10-30): its brand rows and bands don't move, so moving the copy alone left them stranded. `FigmaLayers` can draw a template's copy lockups in a `CopyBlock`; the generator doesn't ask it to. Photos still frame themselves. |
| Cut-outs | `template-lab/codegen.mjs` | A transparent cut-out over the copy (hero 4's people, stood in front of "70% OFF") is removed: the photo under it already has them, so it's the whole photo with the copy over it. As a slot it had also covered the copy and taken every click. |
| Figma export | `src/render/toLayers.ts`, `plugin/src/*` (build `flip-1`), `FigmaLayers.tsx`, generated frames (`data-figma-compiled`) | Read only for compiled boards (a sheet marked `data-figma-compiled`); every other template's export is unchanged, checked on 38 Bump 2, Bump and Flash assets (911 layers identical before and after). A wrapper's blur, shadows and blend reach the layers drawn inside: hero 3's blurred, multiplied shadow boxes had come in as solid boxes, and the plugin now sets blend modes. Board pictures drawn as backgrounds export as images. Trimmed type gets Figma's cap-height trim, and the lab's nudges are taken back off (`data-nudge`): hero 3's copy sat ~26px low. A compiled board's photo exports from the whole transform it's drawn with: its size across and down, its turn, and a mirror (`flip`, which the plugin applies to the image fill). One scale for both axes had lost a turned crop's zoom (email 15's header, the socials), and a mirror had been read as a 180° turn (email 13's). Checked on every photo of all 37 designs: each exported box, turned, covers exactly what's drawn on screen (194 of 194). Grain copies (`data-export="skip"`) aren't layers. |
| Header photos | `template-lab/codegen.mjs` (`backdrop`) | A frame filled with a photo that has things on it (an email's header section, a category tile and its label) has the photo as a slot under them: swappable, and exported (six email bodies had lost their header photo, and "Save on Top Categories" its tiles). |
| Turned marks | `template-lab/codegen.mjs` | A turned shape's mark (hero 2's "NEW BRANDS ADDED" band, hero 3's left marquee) is cut at its own size, not the upright box round it: they had been squashed to a line. |
| Pan | `src/edit/Picture.tsx` | A photo in a mirrored or turned box pans in its own orientation: dragging right moves it right (email 13's mirrored hero panned backwards). |
| Old sessions | `named_sale.ts` (`aliases`) | Sessions made in the proxy trial (`proxy_test`) open as Named Sale. |
| Prune guard | `scripts/figma-pull.ts` | Its clean-up of unused images skips folders, so it can't delete `named-sale/`. |
| Fonts | `public/fonts/` (`fonts.css`), `src/main.tsx`, `src/fonts.ts` | Shipped with the app (internal): Quiet Sans's weights the Adobe kit lacks (600 and 800 declared under the kit's name, so they join it), Tiempos Headline, PestoFresco -- 27 files, 2.9 MB. The mirror is a public repo: these go public with it unless the mirror is made private. |
